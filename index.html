Frequently asked questions

Free terms or defined clusters?

Free terms is inductive: the clusters emerge from the co-occurrence data (Louvain modularity in the app; VOSviewer forms its own when you export). Defined clusters is deductive: you set up to six clusters from your theory or framework, such as SDGs, ESG pillars or GRI topics, and their names and colours are carried into the VOSviewer file. Running the same terms in both modes shows whether your theoretical groups actually appear in the texts.

How do I write the terms?

One term per line, or a block of terms separated by commas, for example hydrogen, ammonia, methanol. Group synonyms with = and separate variants with ;, for example CO2 = carbon dioxide; dióxido de carbono. Use * for endings: emission* matches emission and emissions, but not emitting. Compound terms are searched as whole phrases, hyphens are optional, and case and accents are ignored. Untick “Also search the label itself” when the text before = is only a name.

Document or sentence co-occurrence?

Document (recommended) links two terms once per PDF in which both appear, so a long report that repeats a term cannot dominate the network. Sentence counts every sentence in which the two terms appear together; it suits close readings of a few documents.

Some terms were not found. What now?

The results list every term with zero occurrences. Check spelling, add synonyms or try a wildcard such as govern*. Scanned PDFs contain images, not text, and need OCR first; the file list flags documents with very little text.
